Questions

Is LabScale really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no paywall. Open the app and start working.

Does my data get uploaded?

No. Files you open, tables you type and results you compute stay in your browser. Only optional helpers, such as the Excel reader, are downloaded, and only after you confirm.

Which files can I import?

CSV, TXT and TSV with an import wizard that detects separators, decimal commas, headers, units and dates; Excel workbooks; JSON; HTML tables and XML. You can also paste straight from a spreadsheet.

Can I use my figures in a paper or presentation?

Yes. Graphs export as vector PDF, SVG and EPS or as PNG, JPG, TIFF and WebP; reports export as PDF, Word, HTML and Markdown, and all graphs can go into a PowerPoint file.

How do I choose the right statistical test?

The Stats Advisor looks at your columns, checks assumptions such as normality and equal variances, and recommends tests. Every result shows the numbers behind it.
